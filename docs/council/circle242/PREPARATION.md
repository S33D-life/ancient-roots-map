## Editorial review required

- Blueberries reverse: replace “Vaccinium family” with genus Vaccinium, family
  Ericaceae; review the printed “moorland kinlands” wording. Keep species at genus
  level unless a particular cultivated species is selected.
- Field Maple: pointed/toothed illustrated leaves need review against rounded-lobed
  Acer campestre, both front and reverse; do not relabel another maple silently.
- Flowering Ivy: depict the unlobed adult leaves on flowering shoots, not only
  juvenile lobed leaves. Keep both stages distinct in any reading notes.
- Coco de Mer: illustrated feather-like fronds need review against fan-shaped
  Lodoicea leaves. Qualify the sea-drift story (viable seeds do not disperse like
  coconuts); distinguish seed and fruit. Scientific spelling is Lodoicea maldivica.
- Holly: accompany the bird-food passage with clear human toxicity context. Berries
  are not edible for people; do not infer safety from bird consumption.
- Ely: the illustration is not a documentary likeness. The existing mapped record
  was readable; do not copy its photos, coordinates or contributor information.
  Keep documented tree history distinct from general plane-tree ecology.
- Turkey Tail/Yarrow: traditional-use language is lore, not evidence of treatment
  efficacy or a recommendation to ingest. No medicinal or foraging advice is added.
- Book: imagined cover, not an authorised reproduction; Ken Carey's spiritual
  perspective is not representative Indigenous history. Rights remain separate.
- All ten printed “THE COUNCIL QUESTION” headings need editorial handling. A literal
  transcript must retain what is printed and clearly distinguish a companion prompt.
