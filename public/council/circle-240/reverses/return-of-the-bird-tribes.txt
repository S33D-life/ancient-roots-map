THE LIVING COMPANION

Return of the Bird Tribes is a visionary book by the American writer Ken Carey, first published in the late 1980s as part of his Starseed series. Its author presents it as received spiritual teaching about humanity's bond with the Earth.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Spiritual and literary perspective, not history or ethnography, and not Indigenous teaching. Seek out Indigenous authors for their own traditions. No text is reproduced here.
