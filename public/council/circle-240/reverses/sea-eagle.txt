THE LIVING COMPANION

The white-tailed eagle (Haliaeetus albicilla) is the UK's largest bird of prey, with a wingspan of 2 to 2.4 metres. Adults have a pale head, a huge yellow bill and a short, white, wedge-shaped tail. It eats mainly fish.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Lost as a UK breeding bird by 1916, it returned through reintroduction: Rum from 1975, the Isle of Wight from 2019. Specially protected: watch from afar; never disturb a nest.
