A fragrance for remembering

An evergreen herb of scent, bees and long remembrance.

THE LIVING COMPANION

Rosemary (Salvia rosmarinus, formerly Rosmarinus officinalis) is an aromatic evergreen shrub. Its narrow, dark green leaves are pale and felty beneath. Small blue flowers open from mid-spring to summer, sometimes again in autumn, and feed pollinators.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

A culinary herb. The RHS notes it is a skin allergen, so wear gloves when pruning if your skin is sensitive. Grow it in full sun and well-drained soil; take semi-ripe cuttings in summer.
