THE LIVING COMPANION

One ginkgo (Ginkgo biloba) standing alone on open lawn, its broad trunk dividing low into great spreading limbs. This tree's own age is not known. Its lineage is ancient: fossil ginkgo leaves more than 200 million years old have been found. Native to China, ginkgo is endangered in the wild.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

One individual tree, mapped in the Ancient Friends atlas; its age has not been measured or recorded. Fallen seeds have a fleshy coat that smells of rancid butter as it decays; do not eat or forage them.
