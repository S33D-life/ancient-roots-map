Gold in the hedgerow

Britain's own maple, turning the hedges to gold.

THE LIVING COMPANION

Field maple (Acer campestre) is the UK's only native maple. Its small, dark green leaves have five lobes with rounded teeth, and fade to rich gold in autumn. Its paired winged seeds, tinged pink, are set almost in a straight line.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Field maple leaves are smaller and more rounded than sycamore or Norway maple, and older twigs often bear corky ridges. Its spring flowers feed bees. Not an identification guide.
