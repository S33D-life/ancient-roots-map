THE LIVING COMPANION

Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) seeds are small, flattened and covered in fine hairs. Inside each, a curled embryo lies within its food store under a protective coat. A ripe fruit holds many seeds, each wrapped in jelly-like gel.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Save seed from ripe fruit of an open-pollinated variety; F1 hybrids do not come true. Ferment seeds in their gel for a few days, rinse, dry fully and store cool and dry. Sow indoors at about 18°C.
