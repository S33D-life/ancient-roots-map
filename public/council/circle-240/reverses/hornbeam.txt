THE LIVING COMPANION

European hornbeam (Carpinus betulus) is a deciduous tree with pale grey bark and, with age, a fluted, twisted trunk. Its oval leaves have finely double-toothed edges. Its pale timber is extremely hard, and was once used for butchers' blocks, mill cogs and piano hammers.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Beech or hornbeam? Hornbeam leaves are double-toothed; beech leaves have wavy edges. A clipped hornbeam hedge keeps its dry leaves through winter, giving birds shelter. Not an identification guide.
