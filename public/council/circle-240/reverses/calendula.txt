Pot marigold: easy, edible and bright into autumn.

THE LIVING COMPANION

Pot marigold (Calendula officinalis) is an easy hardy annual with orange or yellow daisy-like flowers from summer into early autumn. Bees and hoverflies visit its open flowers. The petals are edible, with a slightly peppery taste.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Save the curled brown seeds when dry; it often self-seeds. Traditionally used on skin, but clinical evidence is limited and mixed: not medical advice. Not French marigold (Tagetes).
