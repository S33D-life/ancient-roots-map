THE LIVING COMPANION

Horn of plenty (Craterellus cornucopioides), or black trumpet, forms hollow, dark grey funnels with no gills. It grows in leaf litter in broadleaf woods, often under beech or oak, in partnership with tree roots, from late summer to autumn.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Never eat a wild mushroom identified from a picture or this card: foraging needs expert, in-person identification. It dries well for cooking. Medicinal claims are unproven.
