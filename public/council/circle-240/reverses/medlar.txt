Sweetness through patience

An old orchard fruit that sweetens only with waiting.

THE LIVING COMPANION

Medlar (Mespilus germanica) is a small, broad orchard tree of the rose family. Its russet fruits, crowned with long sepals, are picked hard in October or November and then 'bletted': stored until soft, brown and sweet.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Blet on trays, stalks up, not touching, somewhere cool, dark and airy for two or three weeks. Bletted flesh is evenly soft and brown. Discard any fruit with mould, a sour or alcoholic smell, or split skin.
