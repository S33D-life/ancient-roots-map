THE LIVING COMPANION

Ginkgo biloba is the sole living species of an ancient gymnosperm lineage. Its fan-shaped leaves become golden in autumn. The seed has a fleshy outer coat, but that coat is not a botanical fruit.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Ginkgo seeds contain toxins and can cause serious illness, including after cooking. Do not eat them. This image is not foraging guidance.
