THE LIVING COMPANION

The Secret Garden is a 1911 novel by Frances Hodgson Burnett. In the story, Mary Lennox and friends tend a neglected garden. The book offers a literary exploration of attention, friendship, nature and renewal.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Editorial reflection, not a quotation from the novel: “Behind a closed door, life may still be waiting.” This is a literary theme, not a claim that nature cures illness.
