A living witness, holding memory, resilience and the quiet power of deep time.

THE LIVING COMPANION

The Defynnog Yew grows in the churchyard at Defynnog in Powys, Wales. Its great age is difficult to establish precisely, particularly where trunks have hollowed or changed over time. Yews can live for many centuries.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Yew foliage, bark and seeds are poisonous. The red fleshy aril surrounds a toxic seed. Do not eat or forage any part of the tree.
