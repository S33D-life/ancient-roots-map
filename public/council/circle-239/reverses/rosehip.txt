FRUIT COMPANION of yOur Blooming Week

Winter’s gift

A wild gift of resilience, renewal and nourishment, carrying summer’s light into winter.

THE LIVING COMPANION

Rosehips form after rose flowers fade, and many persist into colder months. Some nourish wildlife. Different rose species and preparations have different characteristics. Many rosehips contain vitamin C, though amounts vary.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Do not treat rosehip as a medical treatment. The fine hairs around the seeds can cause irritation. This artwork is not a harvesting guide.
