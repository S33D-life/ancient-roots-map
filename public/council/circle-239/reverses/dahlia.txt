FLOWER COMPANION of yOur Blooming Week

Autumn’s colour

A flower of abundance, creativity and resilient beauty, bright even as the days grow shorter.

THE LIVING COMPANION

Dahlias are cultivated for their many flower shapes and colours. Their underground tubers help them survive between growing seasons. Open-centred forms can offer accessible pollen and nectar; tightly doubled flowers may offer less.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Historical edible or medicinal uses do not make ornamental dahlia plants generally safe to eat. No edible-tuber or treatment recommendation is made here.
