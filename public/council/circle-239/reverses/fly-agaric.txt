THE LIVING COMPANION

Fly agaric (Amanita muscaria) is a striking woodland fungus. Its underground mycelium forms ectomycorrhizal relationships with trees, exchanging resources across the root–fungus partnership. Its appearance has inspired art and stories in particular cultures.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

POISONOUS FUNGUS. Do not eat it or use it in food or home remedies. No preparation, recipe or dosage advice is provided.
