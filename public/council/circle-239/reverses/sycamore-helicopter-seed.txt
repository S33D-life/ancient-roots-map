THE LIVING COMPANION

The sycamore (Acer pseudoplatanus) produces paired winged fruits known as samaras. Each samara carries a seed and spins or glides as it falls, helping it travel away from the parent tree.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

A samara is a winged fruit containing a seed. Do not infer a verified individual tree or wildlife sighting from this illustration.
