9 · Slender-billed Northern Nutcracker

A visitor with seeds

A forest guardian of memory, planting tomorrow’s trees and feeding life through winter.

THE BIRD COMPANION

Northern nutcrackers (Nucifraga caryocatactes) cache seeds for later use. Forgotten seed stores can contribute to the growth of new trees. The name and ecological relationships should be kept distinct from an unverified sighting or subspecies identification.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE
