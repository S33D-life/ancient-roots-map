THE LIVING COMPANION

Ginger (Zingiber officinale) is cultivated for its aromatic rhizome: an underground stem, not a root. It has long been used as a spice and in traditional practices. Some evidence supports particular ginger preparations for certain types of nausea.

LIVING SCIENCE & CARE

Traditional medicinal use is not a promise of treatment. Claims about immune boosting, circulation, inflammation or travel sickness should not be presented as established benefits.
