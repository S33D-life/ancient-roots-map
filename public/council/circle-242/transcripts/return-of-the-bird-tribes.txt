Return of the Bird Tribes
Ken Carey
A visionary book of remembrance and kinship.

ABOUT THE BOOK
Return of the Bird Tribes is a visionary work by Ken Carey that speaks in a lyrical, prophetic voice about humanity, consciousness and the living Earth. It invites the reader to step beyond ordinary habit and remember a more sacred relationship with the world. The book feels less like an argument and more like a summons.

THEMES & GIFTS
Awakening, imagination, service and reverence run through the book like bright threads. Birds appear as messengers of perspective, migration and soul-memory, carrying the sense that another way of being human is still possible. It is a text that asks not only to be read, but listened to.

COUNCIL & CONNECTIONS
For the Council of Life, this book offers companionship in the work of remembering kinship with creation. It speaks to those who feel that culture can turn again toward humility, wonder and participation in a larger living order. Its gift is to re-open the conversation between spirit, Earth and responsibility.
