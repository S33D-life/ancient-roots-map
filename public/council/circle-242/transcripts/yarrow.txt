Yarrow
Achillea millefolium
The field herb that gathers healing light.

NATURAL HISTORY
Yarrow is a hardy perennial of meadows, paths and open ground. Its finely divided, feather-like leaves and flat-topped clusters of tiny white flowers make it one of the most recognisable wild herbs of summer. It spreads by root and seed, returning year after year with a quiet, enduring presence.

ECOLOGY & CONNECTIONS
Yarrow is a generous plant for pollinators, drawing bees, hoverflies, beetles and butterflies to its many small florets. It thrives in lean soils and sunny places, helping weave resilient meadow communities. Its long flowering season makes it a faithful bridge between abundance and decline.

TRADITIONS & LORE
Linked in legend to Achilles, Yarrow has long been associated with mending and protection. It has been used in herbal traditions for wounds, boundaries and blessing, and has also appeared in folklore as a plant of divination and steadfast love. It carries an old memory of healing close at hand.

A REFLECTION
Feather-leaved and many-flowered,
Yarrow keeps the memory of repair.
It gathers small lives around it, and
teaches that healing is often humble,
patient and near.
