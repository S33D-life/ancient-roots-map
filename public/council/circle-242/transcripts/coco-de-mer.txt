Coco de Mer
Lodoicea maldivica
The giant seed that carries an island mystery.

NATURAL HISTORY
Coco de mer is an extraordinary palm found naturally only in the Seychelles. Its immense double-lobed seed is the largest in the plant kingdom, and the tree itself grows slowly, taking many years to flower and fruit. Separate male and female trees shape one of the most remarkable life cycles in the botanical world.

ECOLOGY & CONNECTIONS
This great palm belongs to ancient island forests, where moisture, shade and time all matter. Its broad leaves help shape the forest floor, and its rare seeds speak of a habitat that cannot be hurried. Coco de mer reminds us that some forms of life depend on patience, protection and ecological wholeness.

TRADITIONS & LORE
Before its true home was known, coco de mer seeds were found floating at sea and were once believed to come from trees growing beneath the ocean. The seed became a marvel of trade, story and imagination, and today iit stands as an emblem of rarity and wonder — a treasure best honoured through care rather than possession.

A REFLECTION
Immense and tidal-shaped, Coco de Mer
asks for a slower measure of value.
It teaches that what is rare and ancient
is not ours to seize, but ours to protect.
