Ivy Blossom
Hedera helix
The late flower that keeps the season open.

NATURAL HISTORY
Ivy is an evergreen climber and woodland companion, but its flowering phase is one of the quiet wonders of autumn. When many plants are fading, Ivy lifts pale green-yellow flower clusters into the late light. Its glossy leaves, wandering stems and seasonal transformation make it both familiar and full of surprise.

ECOLOGY & CONNECTIONS
Ivy blossom is one of the most valuable late nectar sources for bees, hoverflies and wasps, and its berries later feed birds through winter. Dense ivy cover also offers nesting shelter and refuge. It stands at the turning of the year, feeding life when other doors are closing.

TRADITIONS & LORE
In folklore, Ivy has long symbolised endurance, fidelity and the power of life to persist through dark seasons. It is often paired with Holly as a balancing companion — evergreen with evergreen, shelter with shelter. Ivy teaches the strength of climbing, clinging and quietly remaining.

A REFLECTION
Late-flowering and ever-climbing,
Ivy keeps the season open. It reminds us
that life does not end where summer fades;
it gathers itself and offers again.
